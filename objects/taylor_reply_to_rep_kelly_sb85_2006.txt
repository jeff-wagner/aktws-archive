Dear Mr. Kelly: 

Thank you for your letter.  The Alaska Chapter of The Wildlife Society remains opposed per the reasons outlined in our letter.  I am unaware of any correspondence, regulation, or policy that indicated the level of protection currently associated with the Dalton Highway corridor was meant to be temporary.  Indeed, after 20+ years, I would expect this protection to be assumed permanent.  Further, the proposed bill has not provided any funding for state, federal, and borough agencies to monitor the potential effects of off-road vehicles and snowmachines to wildlife and wildlife habitat resources.  In summary, the proposed legislation does not ensure that the  concerns we have described in our correspondence will be addressed.  I appreciate your time and effort to consider our comments.

Dr. Taylor, thank you for your detailed comments concerning SB85.  You
make several excellent points.  I am familiar with the Dalton and must
tell you respectfully that I support improved access from the Dalton.
Current limitations are too restrictive, were not meant to be permanent,
and fence off millions of acres from all but the most fit and
resourceful among us.  That puts increased pressure on the fewer and
fewer areas available for enjoyment by multiple use folks that live in
and visit our great state.  I am one who would benefit from lockup.  We
have a plane on floats, wheels and skis and remote cabins and equipment
to get as far from another human as is possible on this planet.  Many
are not so fortunate.  We can do Dalton access right, address your valid
concerns and still create incredible opportunities up north for those
who are currently denied reasonable access.  Thank you.  Mike
